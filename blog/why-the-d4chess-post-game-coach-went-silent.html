Why was the D4Chess post-game coach silent — or talking over itself?

An empty coach payload and overlapping audio are the same race: two async paths, no owner of the channel. We gated playback on a complete result.

Shane Foster  ·  Founder, Haven Command LLC  ·  August 24, 2026

What problem did we actually hit?

Problem

After a game on D4Chess, the coach is supposed to talk. Sometimes it didn’t. Sometimes it did — twice — two clips stepping on each other so the lesson became noise. Players do not file a bug titled “async race.” They leave.

The empty case and the overlap case look opposite. They are the same design hole. One path fetches the post-game analysis. Another path starts audio. Nothing required a complete, exclusive payload before sound left the speaker.

This is ordinary production work. It is also the work a typical AI hire skips after the demo talks. Haven Command ships products it operates; the company notices when the coach ghosts a player who just lost on time.

How did Haven Command solve it?

Solution

Playback now waits for a complete coach result — not a resolved promise that happens to be empty. If the analysis is not there, we retry or show a quiet failure, not a blank “done.”

Audio has one owner. A new clip cancels or queues the previous one. We do not let two coach voices share the channel because a second request arrived 200 milliseconds later. The lesson is a single turn, like a human coach who finishes a sentence.

Same discipline shows up in TrustLoopAI SMS loops and TheraScribe note generation: do not start the next side-effect until the last one is finished or explicitly abandoned.

If two requests can talk, they will talk at once — or neither will. Give the channel one owner.

For clients

Voice, streaming, and “notify me when the model is done” features fail in production the same way: races, empty payloads, retries that stack. Questions this write-up answers

Why did the post-game coach sometimes say nothing?

The UI was ready to play a lesson before the coach payload existed. An empty result still counted as “done,” so the player got silence after a real game.

Why did two coach voices overlap?

A second request could start audio before the first clip finished or cancelled. Nothing owned the speaker.

What is the client version of this bug?

Any voice or streaming AI product with retry, prefetch, or “play when ready” has this race. Ship a single owner for the channel.
