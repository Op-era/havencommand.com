Why did the week’s posts never go out after a “working” demo?

The calendar looked connected in the meeting. A week later nothing published. After the fix, the owner can grant, renew, and revoke the connection — and the week actually goes out.

Shane Foster · Founder, Haven Command LLC

What happens when the calendar looks connected and nothing posts?

A calendar that “just posts” looks connected in the meeting. In production the week does not go out. Nobody owns publish. Captions still get copied by hand.

A connection the owner cannot grant, renew, or revoke is not an integration. It is a hostage that fails on a Tuesday morning.

What would that have felt like on AutoSocial?

AutoSocial is supposed to turn one idea into a week of posts. A demo-grade connection would have looked fine in a meeting and gone silent the first week a real calendar had to ship.

The experience is brutal: you paid for publish, and nothing published.

What did we change?

The week goes out. The owner can see what is connected, reconnect when it expires, and revoke access. The product does not pretend it is still posting when it is not.

How the connection is granted is not a public recipe. The outcome is on the product note.

If it only posts during the demo, you do not have a product. You have a meeting.

Why does this show up in custom software?

Automation and vendor APIs fail after the handshake, not during it. Haven Command already shipped a live calendar that still publishes. That is the same class of work as a commissioned integration: the tool that does not talk to the other tool, and then has to keep talking. See integrations and APIs.
