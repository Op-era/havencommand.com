How do you stop an AI agent from overwriting a live script?

Typical AI hires let the model write the work product. The draft disappears. Haven Command locks the live script, merges only on Approve, and keeps undo until save — because a green click must not cremate the document.

Shane Foster  ·  Founder, Haven Command LLC  ·  August 10, 2026

What do typical AI hires fail at here?

Fail

They treat the live document as a chat buffer. “AI that edits our docs” lands as dual-write: the human types, the model writes through the same record, and the last token wins. There is no lock, no approve, no undo. Trust dies the first time a contract, a clinical note, or a screenplay vanishes into a helpful pass.

Even shops that add an Approve button often make that click final. One green light, history gone. That is irreversible AI. Legal and editorial buyers will not forgive it.

What broke in production?

Broke

ScriptForge AI is a screenwriting product, not a chat window with a .fountain export. Writers stay in a live script for hours. Agents track structure and continuity. Those two facts collide the moment an agent write lands on the same document the human is typing.

The first failure: you asked for a polish on scene three and the live draft you were finishing is gone. The second, after lock-and-Approve shipped: a writer would green-light a rewrite, walk to the kitchen, come back, and realize scene two now belonged to a different movie — with no undo. Approve had become a cremation.

What did Haven Command change?

Change

The live script locks. Agents do not write through it. They propose. The writer hits green to approve or red to throw it away. Until then, the draft on the page is the draft on the page.

Approved rewrites stay in history until the writer saves. Approve means merge. Save means commit. Until save, undo walks the rewrite back like any other edit. The model stays a petitioner. The writer gets time inside the tool, not a lecture about git.

Same rule as grounding the D4Chess coach: the model does not get to be the ledger. Lock-and-Approve plus reversible history now sit in the systems library.

If the model can write the document, it can destroy the document. Lock the live copy. Do not let Approve cremate it.
