What does TrustLoopAI do when DeepSeek fails in a live SMS loop?

Typical AI hires demo one model. When the vendor blinks, the customer is sitting on a dead thread. Haven Command fails over to Gemini Flash and keeps the loop — plus a transport that actually stays up.

Shane Foster  ·  Founder, Haven Command LLC  ·  June 29, 2026

What do typical AI hires fail at here?

Fail

They demo one model. The slide has a single logo. When that vendor errors, times out, or changes a quota, the conversation dies. There is no failover, no transport story, no log — just “try again later” on a customer’s phone.

Buyers already know the sentence: “It worked in the demo then fell over in production.” The model vendor blinked. Nobody owned the loop.

What broke in production?

Broke

TrustLoopAI is a customer-engagement loop over SMS. If the primary model — DeepSeek, in this setup — errors or times out, the customer is still sitting on a text thread. “Try again later” is not a conversation.

Separately, the app has to talk to Supabase from Node 20 on Fly. The easy HTTP assumption is not always the path that stays open. A “connected” product that cannot reach its own database is a demo with a phone number.

What did Haven Command change?

Change

When DeepSeek fails, the loop falls over to Gemini Flash and continues. The customer does not need the roster. They need the next useful message. Failover is logged for cost and quality. It is not an autopsy in the text thread.

On the data hop, the product uses a websocket transport that works for Supabase on that Node 20 / Fly combination. Plumbing is the product. Same instinct as grounding D4Chess: an upstream hole must not become fluent nonsense or a dead line.

A fallback model is not a luxury. It is how an SMS loop survives someone else’s outage.
