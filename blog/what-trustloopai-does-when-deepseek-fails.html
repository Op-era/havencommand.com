Why does an SMS conversation die when the model vendor fails?

TrustLoopAI is in development — not a live product. While building the SMS loop, a vendor failure went silent mid-thread. The decision: the next message still has to arrive. The person on the phone never sees the autopsy.

Shane Foster · Founder, Haven Command LLC

Is TrustLoopAI live?

No. TrustLoopAI is a Haven Command project in development: customer-engagement software built around SMS loops — reminders, follow-ups, and conversations that have to keep going. There is no public product URL. These notes are from the build, not from a launched commercial site.

That honesty matters. A product that is not up does not get to borrow the word “live.”

What happens when the conversation dies mid-thread?

The slide has a single logo. When that vendor blinks, the conversation dies. The person on the phone gets “try again later.”

The sentence is already known: “It worked in the demo then fell over in production.” Nobody owned the loop.

What did the build hit?

While building the SMS path, a vendor failure went silent mid-thread. The person we are building for — a salon, a clinic, a coach — would still be sitting on that thread. A connected-looking product that cannot finish the next message is a demo with a phone number.

They would not care which logo failed. They would care that the business went silent.

What is the design decision?

The next useful message still has to arrive. The thread does not get an autopsy. The loop has to survive someone else’s bad day.

How vendors are chosen or routed is not on this page. The decision is the public part. Current status lives with the other in-development work on products.

The person is still on the thread. “Try again later” is not a product.

Why does this show up in custom software?

Commissioned automation fails the same way: one vendor in the meeting, silence on Tuesday. Haven Command is designing that loop before TrustLoopAI is public — and already ran related reliability failures on live products like D4Chess and AutoSocial. The products that are up are the proof. This note is the thinking.
