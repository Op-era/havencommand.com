Production notes on shipping AI systems — not demos

These notes are the paper trail of Haven Command LLC: one bottleneck per post from live products. They are also a map of what typical AI hires fail at — demos that die on deploy, model answers ungrounded in a system of record, AI overwrite without approve/undo, OTP theater instead of operable login, and state that breaks on restart. Haven Command has already worked past those modes. Hire Haven Command for the same class of work.
