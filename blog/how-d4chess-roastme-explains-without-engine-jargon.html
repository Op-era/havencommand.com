How do you make engine analysis go viral without the jargon?

RoastMe is an explainability layer: same verified line, human language. People share a roast. They do not share +1.4 at move 23.

Shane Foster  ·  AI Integrations Engineer, Haven Command LLC  ·  August 17, 2026

What problem did we actually hit?

Problem

Engine output is correct and unreadable. A principal variation and an eval tell a club player almost nothing they will repeat to a friend. On D4Chess we needed a format people would actually finish — and sometimes share — without turning the product into a comedy account that lies about the board.

The temptation is to let the model “just roast the game.” That path reopens the hallucination hole: a punchline about a queen blunder that was not in the game. Explainability that invents the thing it explains is marketing, not a trainer.

How did Haven Command solve it?

Solution

RoastMe is a second voice on the same verified line. The engine still computes. The coach still has to stay inside the PV. RoastMe is allowed to be short, sharp, and human — “you hung the exchange on a hope chess check” — as long as that sentence maps to a real moment in the game.

Jargon gets translated, not deleted. The player can still open the line. The roast is the doorway. That is the product decision: distribution without a second, ungrounded model.

I wrote more about grounding in why the coach cannot invent Stockfish lines. RoastMe is the same architecture with a different register.

Virality is a distribution problem. Honesty is still an engineering problem.

Questions this write-up answers

What is RoastMe on D4Chess?

A shareable, human-language explanation of a real game, grounded on the same verified engine line as the coach — not a separate joke bot that invents blunders.

Does a roast mean the analysis is less accurate?

No. Tone is the layer. The moves still have to exist in the engine output. Funny and false is just another hallucination.

What is the client lesson?

If you want adoption, translate the system of record. Do not replace it with a personality.
