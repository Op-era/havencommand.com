Why did the D4Chess coach invent lines Stockfish never played?

Typical AI hires ship a fluent coach with no board. Haven Command grounds every sentence on a verified Stockfish principal variation — then translates it so a non-expert can trust it.

Shane Foster  ·  Founder, Haven Command LLC  ·  August 31, 2026

What do typical AI hires fail at here?

Fail

They treat the model as the source of truth. A demo coach sounds smart because it talks like a specialist. It has no board, no ledger, no engine. The buyer finds out later: staff still paste the answer into the real system by hand, then quietly correct the lies.

That is the hire you are trying not to repeat. If the product “explains” a chess game, a SOAP note, a spreadsheet, or a customer thread, the model is a narrator. The system of record is the record.

What broke in production?

Broke

On D4Chess the coach is supposed to walk a player through their game — the mistake they just made, the line the engine actually prefers. A language model is very good at sounding like that coach. It is much worse at staying inside the position on the board.

The failure was not a crash. It was worse: a fluent, confident explanation of a variation Stockfish never produced. A player who trusts that sentence studies a ghost. That is not a demo bug. That is a product that trains the wrong thing — and a buyer who will never trust the next coach you ship.

What did Haven Command change?

Change

The company stopped treating the model as an oracle. Stockfish computes the principal variation. That PV is checked. Only then does the coach speak — and only about those moves, that eval, that position. If the engine data is missing, the coach stays quiet instead of filling silence with chess-shaped noise.

Explainability is the same pipeline, not a second ungrounded bot. RoastMe is a human-language layer on the verified line so a club player can trust the lesson without reading +1.4 at move 23. Tone can change. The board cannot.

That grounded-coach pipeline now lives in the Haven Command systems library — next to the same “record first, talk second” pattern on ScriptForge and TrustLoopAI. Client work plugs in. It does not start from a prompt that says “please be accurate.”

The engine is the source of record. The model is allowed to talk about the record — not invent a prettier one.
