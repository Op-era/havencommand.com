How do you productize referrals and PAYG caps without ops chaos?

Usage without a ceiling is an outage you invoice later. TrustLoopAI treats referrals, billing, and PAYG caps as product, not a spreadsheet after the fact.

Shane Foster  ·  AI Integrations Engineer, Haven Command LLC  ·  June 22, 2026

What problem did we actually hit?

Problem

Once TrustLoopAI could talk to real customers, usage became a product question. Referrals bring people in. Pay-as-you-go feels fair. Neither is fair if a single loop, a retry storm, or a generous friend can run the meter without a ceiling.

I have no interest in “we will invoice the surprise later.” That is how a small SMS product becomes an ops incident with a Stripe notification.

How did Haven Command solve it?

Solution

Caps and referral rules live in the product. PAYG has a ceiling. Referrals have terms the software can enforce. Billing is not a side quest I remember after a long week.

This pairs with model failover: a fallback that keeps the loop alive also keeps the meter running. Failover without a cap is how you donate margin to an outage.

If the only cap is your credit card alert, you did not finish the product.

Why does this help someone hiring custom AI work?

For clients

Companies hiring AI integration work already know usage can escape. Founders funding a build often skip the meter to ship the demo. I ship the meter, then keep referral and PAYG-cap billing in the Haven Command systems library so the next usage product plugs into a proven ceiling instead of a spreadsheet.

If you need a usage-shaped AI product — SMS, notes, content — with billing that cannot wake you up, start at the systems library note or contact@havencommand.com.

Questions this write-up answers

Why do PAYG AI products need caps?

Models, SMS, and retries all cost money. Without a cap, a loop or a bad actor becomes an unbounded bill — yours or the customer’s.

What did TrustLoopAI ship?

Referral and billing rules with explicit pay-as-you-go ceilings, enforced in the product, not reconciled in a weekend spreadsheet.

Is this “AI work”?

Yes. A production integration includes the meter. Clients do not hire a model; they hire a system they can afford to leave on.
