What happens when the product opens on a prompt?

Stake

The product opens on a prompt. “Describe what you want.” When the model stalls or is wrong, there is nowhere to work. The human does not own the artifact. They wait on a kiosk.

That is how a team pays for AI and gets prompts, not a workflow. The job still happens in another tool — if any output arrives at all.

What broke for the customer?

Broke

On ScriptForge AI that empty state almost shipped: you sat down to type a scene, and the tool asked you to prompt. A writer with a deadline and a slow model has no floor. They cannot even start badly.

A generated-only surface is a demo that only works when the model is warm and impressive.

After
