Market Opportunity

Chess Training Software

$2.1B
↑ 12.4% CAGR through 2030
600M+ chess players globally. Chess.com alone has 150M registered users. D4Chess targets the serious improvement segment — club players, tournament competitors, and coaching students underserved by generic platforms.

Therapy Documentation Tools

$1.8B
↑ 19.3% CAGR through 2030
~180,000 licensed therapists in the US. Top competitors (SimplePractice, TherapyNotes) charge $59–$99/month and do not offer AI note generation. TheraScribe enters at a lower price with a feature they don't have.

AI Writing Tools

$3.8B
↑ 26.1% CAGR through 2030
ScriptForge targets professional screenwriters and novelists — a niche where general tools (ChatGPT, Claude) lack the specialized agent structure screenwriters need for long-form consistency and format compliance.

Indie Games Market

$5.5B
↑ 14.2% CAGR through 2031
Cognition and HowGamersGame target the $10–$25 premium indie tier on Steam and browser platforms. Successful indie titles in the horror-puzzle genre regularly sell 50,000–500,000 copies at this price point.

SMB Scheduling & CRM

$11.4B
↑ 9.8% CAGR through 2030
TrustLoop AI targets salons, clinics, and coaches that pay $49–$149/month for tools like Mindbody, Acuity, and Square Appointments — but want AI-driven retention rather than passive scheduling.
