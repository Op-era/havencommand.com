Your business doesn’t have to fit the software.

The software can fit your business. That is the whole pitch, without the pitch. If a catalog tool already does the job, use it. If you are forcing your process into five workarounds and a spreadsheet, you are past the buy-vs-build conversation.

Haven Command takes commissioned builds: custom web applications, business software, AI-powered apps, automation, APIs and integrations, customer portals, internal tools, SaaS products, interactive apps, games, and specialized software that is simply odd. Unusual is not a problem. Vague “add AI” is.

What custom software can look like

Custom Applications

Software written for one company or one product idea. Internal tools, operations systems, and the thing your team currently runs in email.

AI Applications

A model doing a named job inside a real product: coaching, drafting, notes, routing, summaries. The week after launch is the product.

Automation

Business automation that publishes, reminds, files, or moves work between systems without someone babysitting the demo.

Web & Customer Applications

Custom web applications and customer portals: accounts, roles, the work the customer came to do, and a screen that actually comes up.

Integrations & APIs

Connect the tools you already pay for. Auth, webhooks, vendor APIs, and the failure modes that show up on a Tuesday.

Digital Products

SaaS development and software product development — including games and specialized tools. If it has users, it has to survive them.

How a build runs

The short version. The longer one is on How We Work.

01 Discover

Name the bottleneck

What are you trying to build, and what problem does it have to solve? If it is not specific enough to commission, we will say so.

02 Design

Shape the system

The workflow, the screens, the edges, the things that must not break. Enough design to build — not a six-month theater piece.

03 Build

Make the software

Custom software, integrations, and the unglamorous parts: auth, data, permissions, the empty states, the admin door.

04 Deploy

Put it in production

A deploy that comes up. A login the operator can open. A job that still runs after the meeting.

05 Improve

Keep it after first contact

Users find the real product. Iteration, reliability, and the next constraint. Software is not finished at launch.

Questions people actually ask

When should I build custom software instead of buying a subscription?

Buy when a product already does the job. Build when the off-the-shelf tool almost works — except for the part that is your actual work. The workflow, the exception, the integration, or the product you need to own.

What kinds of custom software does Haven Command build?

Custom web apps, business software, AI-powered apps, automation, APIs and integrations, customer portals, internal tools, SaaS products, interactive apps, games, and specialized software that does not fit a template.

How does a project run?

Discover → Design → Build → Deploy → Improve. The point is a working system you can use. Details on How We Work.

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on the shape of the problem. A focused workflow tool is a different job than a multi-sided product. We will say if the scope is a fit or too open-ended. There are no catalog prices here — those would be fiction.

Is this a freelancer hunting small coding jobs?

No. Haven Command LLC is a software and product engineering studio. Shane Foster is the founder. The live products on this site are company systems.

Do you only build AI products?

No. AI is one tool. Sometimes the right build is a portal, an API, a game, or a boring internal tool that has to stay up.

What do you need from us to start?

A name, a way to reach you, what you are trying to build, and the problem you are trying to solve. Optional: a qualitative sense of scope. Start a Project.
