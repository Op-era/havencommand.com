Haven Command LLC, at a Glance

Haven Command LLC is a software, education, and technology company that combines serious development with a passion for games. Our mission is to make learning cool and to build high-quality software, applications, and games that meaningfully improve people's lives.

We develop engaging educational tools, productivity software, and immersive entertainment experiences at the intersection of technology and creativity. The company is led by Shane Foster, Founder, CEO, and Managing Member.

Where We Market

Our marketing runs on a daily content engine built around D4Chess. Every day, a new chess puzzle video goes out on TikTok with music, and every post is cross-posted to Instagram, X, Facebook, and Threads. Each puzzle points back to its full solution page on d4chess.club, so the content brings people to the actual product. We also publish regular long-form articles on the D4Chess blog.
