SECTION 2 — INTEREST
================================================================================

2.1  INTEREST RATE

     The outstanding Principal balance shall accrue simple interest at the
     annual rate of ___________________ (_____%) per year from the Funding Date.

     Monthly interest accrual = Outstanding Principal × 4% ÷ 12

     Example: $5,000 Principal × 4% ÷ 12 = $16.67 interest per month.

     NOTE: 4% is set at or above the IRS Applicable Federal Rate (AFR) to
     avoid imputed interest treatment. If the IRS AFR rises above 4% in a
     future year, the Parties agree to amend this rate in writing to match
     the then-current mid-term AFR, if required.

2.2  INTEREST ACCRUES FROM FUNDING DATE

     Interest begins accruing on the Funding Date regardless of when
     payments begin. This compensates Investor for the time value of money
     during the pre-payment period.

2.3  TOTAL AMOUNT OWED

     "Total Amount Owed" as of any date = Principal + all simple interest
     accrued from the Funding Date through that date.

     No payments are required before the Payment Trigger defined in
     Section 4.1. However, interest continues to accrue during that period,
     so the Total Amount Owed grows until repayment begins.

     Illustrative example (for a $5,000 investment, 12 months to trigger):

       Interest accrued pre-trigger (12 months): $200.00
       Total Amount Owed at trigger:             $5,200.00
       Monthly payment (24-month schedule):      $216.67/month
       Total paid to Investor:                   ~$5,220 (principal + interest)

SECTION 3 — PROFITABILITY DEFINITION
================================================================================

3.1  GROSS MONTHLY REVENUE

     "Gross Monthly Revenue" means all cash actually received by the Company
     during a calendar month from customers, subscriptions, licenses, and
     product sales — net of refunds and chargebacks processed in that month.
     It does not include loans, owner contributions, or investor proceeds.

3.2  ACTUAL MONTHLY CASH EXPENSES

     "Actual Monthly Cash Expenses" means all cash actually paid out by the
     Company during a calendar month for hosting, software subscriptions,
     contractors, advertising, and other direct operating costs.

     This definition intentionally excludes:
       (a) Deferred compensation obligations not yet paid in cash;
       (b) Non-cash accounting entries.

     The intent is to measure real cash flow, not accounting net income.

3.3  CASH FLOW POSITIVE THRESHOLD

     The Company is "Cash Flow Positive" when:

       Gross Monthly Revenue > Actual Monthly Cash Expenses

     for TWO (2) consecutive calendar months ("Profitable Period").

     This definition is simple and objectively verifiable from the Company's
     bank statements alone.

SECTION 4 — PAYMENT TRIGGER AND REPAYMENT TERMS
================================================================================

4.1  PAYMENT TRIGGER

     Repayment shall begin on the first day of the calendar month following
     the end of the Profitable Period defined in Section 3.3
     ("Payment Trigger Date").

     The Company shall notify Investor in writing within 5 business days of
     the Payment Trigger Date being reached.

4.2  REPAYMENT SCHEDULE

     Beginning on the Payment Trigger Date, the Company shall pay Investor
     a fixed monthly installment calculated as follows:

       Monthly Payment = Total Amount Owed on the Payment Trigger Date ÷ 24

     Payments continue on the same calendar day each month until the Total
     Amount Owed is paid in full or the 24-month schedule is complete,
     whichever comes first.

     The 24-month schedule is designed to give the Company a predictable
     obligation while ensuring Investor receives full repayment within a
     reasonable timeframe.

4.3  PAYMENT DEFERRAL IN LOW-REVENUE MONTHS

     If in any given month after the Payment Trigger Date the Company's
     Gross Monthly Revenue falls below its Actual Monthly Cash Expenses
     (i.e., the Company is no longer Cash Flow Positive), the Company may
     defer that month's payment.

     Deferred payments carry forward and are added to the remaining balance.
     The repayment schedule extends by one month for each month deferred.
     Deferred months do not cancel the obligation — they only delay it.

     The Company shall notify Investor in writing within 5 business days of
     any deferred payment.

4.4  MATURITY DATE (LONGSTOP)

     Regardless of whether the Payment Trigger has been met, the full
     outstanding Total Amount Owed shall become due and payable no later
     than FIVE (5) YEARS from the Funding Date ("Maturity Date").

     If the Total Amount Owed has not been repaid in full by the Maturity
     Date, Investor may demand repayment in writing. Upon such demand, the
     Company shall repay the full remaining balance within NINETY (90) DAYS.

     The Maturity Date provision exists to ensure Investor has a definite
     outside date by which repayment must occur, regardless of the
     Company's revenue performance.

4.5  EARLY REPAYMENT

     The Company may repay the Total Amount Owed in full or in part at any
     time, without penalty. Partial early payments reduce the outstanding
     Principal, which reduces subsequent interest accrual.

4.6  FINAL PAYMENT AND TERMINATION

     Upon receipt of the final payment, this Agreement terminates
     automatically. The Company shall provide Investor written confirmation
     that the obligation is satisfied within 10 business days of the final
     payment.

4.7  PAYMENT METHOD

     All payments shall be made by ACH transfer, check, or other mutually
     agreed written method. Investor shall provide current banking
     information in writing. Changes to banking information require written
     notice at least 10 business days before the next payment.
