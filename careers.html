Internships at Haven Command

Haven Command LLC is a custom software and digital product studio. The internships below support shipped products and original software — company work, not a personal freelance shop, and not a games-only studio.

Game Developer Intern

Assist in the development of our gaming projects. Responsibilities include coding, debugging, and implementing game features, as well as collaborating with the design and art teams.

2D/3D Modeling and Topology Intern

Help create visual assets for our games: modeling and texturing characters, environments, and props, and optimizing assets for performance.

Game Design Intern

Assist in game concepts, mechanics, and levels. Responsibilities include designing and prototyping gameplay features, balancing systems, and reviewing player feedback.

Audio Engineer Intern

Assist in recording and editing audio assets, implementing sound and music, and collaborating with design to support the player experience.

Marketing Intern

Assist in promoting our products and talking to the community: materials, social channels, and marketing data.
