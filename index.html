We build our own products.

That is not a branding line. It is how the studio stays honest. The same problems show up whether the software is ours or yours: deploy, auth, databases, users, billing, UX, maintenance, APIs, AI that has to be reliable, production failures, scaling, and the next iteration after someone actually uses it.

Haven Command already ran those problems on live products. D4Chess had to tell the truth about a game. ScriptForge had to leave the draft with the writer. AutoSocial had to boot and publish. TheraScribe had to produce a note without keeping the session. Emoji Isle had to come back as one world. That is the evidence — not a claim about being the best in a category.
