Who is Haven Command?

Haven Command LLC designs and ships production AI products: D4Chess, ScriptForge AI, TrustLoopAI, TheraScribe, and AutoSocial. The same class of system — one named bottleneck, the whole loop, kept in production — is available as commissioned work. Founded by Shane Foster.

The blog is a dated record of what those products had to survive: a coach that invented the record, a week that never published, a conversation that died mid-thread. The products are the evidence.

What does the work look like after it ships?

The week after launch is the product. Haven Command’s systems already ran those weeks:

  • A deploy that comes up. AutoSocial looked live and painted black. A “connected” calendar never posted the week. After the work, the product boots — and the week goes out.
  • A lesson that matches the record. The D4Chess coach invented a game that never happened. After the work, the lesson matches the real game — or the coach stays quiet.
  • A draft the human still owns. ScriptForge writers lost live pages to “helpful” passes. After the work, the writer still owns the script.
  • A door the operator can open. The owner could not get into the product they operate. After the work, the person on the hook can still open the door.
  • A named bottleneck, not “add AI.” Haven Command takes one workflow with a shipped loop you can point at.
  • A shared world that comes back. Emoji Isle players returned to different islands. After the work, everyone is in the same world again.

Same class of outcome on a commissioned build: D4Chess coaches from actual mistakes, TheraScribe turns a session into a note the clinician can use, TrustLoopAI keeps an SMS conversation alive when a vendor has a bad day.

Commissioned work starts from the Haven Command systems library — reusable, integration-ready patterns from products the company already shipped. Not a public catalog. Not a walkthrough. Faster than a one-off demo. Evidence: blog.

Questions about Haven Command

What does Haven Command actually ship?

A finished production system: the model, the product around it, and the workflow it replaces — including auth, billing, deploy, publish, and admin — so the job runs after the meeting. Live evidence includes D4Chess, ScriptForge AI, TrustLoopAI, TheraScribe, and AutoSocial.

What does a production AI product have to survive?

The week after launch: a screen that actually comes up, a lesson that matches the real record, a draft the human still owns, a door the operator can open, and shared work that comes back as one world. Haven Command’s products already ran those weeks.

Can Haven Command build something like D4Chess for my business?

Yes. D4Chess is a focused production AI product. Haven Command custom-builds the same class of system for a different domain: one bottleneck, end-to-end.

Does Haven Command work with founders or only companies?

Both. Founders funding a build and companies commissioning an AI integrations partner. The fit is a funded, specific problem — not an open-ended “add AI” exploration.

What kinds of AI integrations does Haven Command take on?

Workflow automation, custom AI products, and systems that connect models to existing tools — SMS loops, clinical notes, content pipelines, coaching products. If the job is one named bottleneck, that’s in range.

Is Haven Command LLC a company or a freelance practice?

Haven Command LLC is the company. Shane Foster is the founder. The products on this page are company systems, not a personal portfolio of one-off freelance jobs.

How do I start a custom AI product build?

Email contact@havencommand.com. Name the workflow you want running and whether you are a founder funding a build or a company commissioning the work.

Where can I see how Haven Command solves production AI problems?

On the Haven Command blog: what broke for customers in live products, and what the experience became after the fix. The products are evidence — not walkthroughs of internals.

What is the Haven Command systems library?

An internal set of reusable, integration-ready patterns from products Haven Command already shipped. Custom work starts from proven outcomes, not a one-off demo. It is not a public product. See the note above and the blog.
