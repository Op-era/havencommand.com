AutoSocial

Automated social and content workflow. Feed it one idea; get a week of platform-ready posts that are supposed to go out — and do.

Open AutoSocial All products

What it is

A Haven Command workflow product for turning an idea into scheduled, platform-adapted social content. The public app is at autosocial.havencommand.com. It is software we operate, not a client case study.

The problem

A calendar that “just posts” looks connected in the meeting. In production the week does not go out, captions still get copied by hand, and nobody owns publish. A connection the operator cannot grant, renew, or revoke is not an integration.

What Haven Command built

The loop from idea to a week on a calendar, including the unglamorous parts: a deploy that actually boots, an owner who can still log in, and publish that either goes out or stops pretending it is connected.

Key capabilities

  • Idea-to-week content workflow
  • Platform-ready drafts, not one generic caption for every network
  • Scheduling and publish through real platform APIs
  • Operator login that has to work in production

What was hard — at the product level

Three failures already showed up in public: a “finished” deploy that painted black, an owner locked out of the product they operate, and a week that never published after a working-looking demo. After the work, the product boots, the operator can get in, and the week goes out. Notes: black screen, login, publish.

Status

Live at autosocial.havencommand.com.
