TheraScribe

AI-assisted documentation for therapy sessions. Record, review a transcript, and copy a SOAP, DAP, or BIRP note — without storing PHI.

Open therascribe.online All products

What it is

A clinical-notes product Haven Command built for licensed therapists. The clinician records a session in the browser, reviews a transcript, and leaves with a formatted note they can paste into their EHR. The public product is at therascribe.online.

The problem

Charting eats the evening. Generic speech tools stumble on clinical language. Stored recordings create a liability the practice did not ask for. A note that takes as long to clean up as it did to write is not a workflow.

What Haven Command built

A session-to-note loop with medical-grade transcription, speaker labels, confidence flagging, and SOAP / DAP / BIRP output. Session content is not kept: audio is discarded after transcription; transcripts are discarded after the note. That constraint is the product as much as the note is.

Key capabilities

  • In-browser session recording
  • Clinical-vocabulary transcription with low-confidence words flagged
  • Speaker diarization for who said what
  • SOAP, DAP, and BIRP notes the clinician can copy into an EHR
  • Zero PHI storage on Haven Command servers, as the product describes it

What was hard — at the product level

The note has to be usable, and the session cannot become a dataset we keep. Those two constraints pull in opposite directions. TheraScribe is evidence we will design the workflow around the constraint instead of waving at compliance. This page is not a HIPAA legal opinion and not an architecture walkthrough.
