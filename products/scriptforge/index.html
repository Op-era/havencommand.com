ScriptForge AI

AI-assisted screenwriting. Agents can help with story structure, continuity, and rewrites. The live draft stays with the writer.

What it is

A writing product Haven Command built for people who stay in a script for hours. Drafting, rewriting, story-bible and continuity work, table-read style passes, and production planning live in the same workspace. The public site is the product — not a marketing shell in front of a demo.

The problem

Most “AI writing” tools start you in a prompt and treat the document as disposable. A helpful pass that overwrites the scene you were finishing is not a feature. Writers who finish drafts need a blank page they can type into immediately, and a model that cannot cremate last week’s work.

What Haven Command built

A screenwriting workspace with AI in the room — structure, continuity, character notes — and rules around the live script. Suggestions are suggestions. Nothing replaces the draft until the writer means it, and a bad accept is still recoverable until they choose to keep the new version.

Key capabilities

  • Screenplay drafting in a dedicated workspace
  • Rewrite and revision flows that do not silently eat the live pages
  • Story-structure and continuity assistance
  • A blank script tab so the writing day does not wait on the model
  • Production-adjacent planning for scripts and video work

What was hard — at the product level

Companies ask for AI that edits documents. What they need is a product that does not steal the work. ScriptForge already burned that failure on a live writing product. The internals stay internal. The outcome is on Insights.
