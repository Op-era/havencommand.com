About

A software studio with a public product lab.

Haven Command LLC builds custom software for clients and operates its own products. Founded by Shane Foster. Based in Fresno, California.

Who is Haven Command?

Two sides of the same shop. First: custom software for problems that off-the-shelf products cannot solve — web apps, business software, AI-powered applications, automation, APIs, portals, internal tools, SaaS, games, and the specialized thing that does not have a category yet. Second: our own products, kept in production, used as proof that we already sit with deploy, auth, data, users, billing, UX, APIs, and the week after launch.

That is a studio, not a freelancer hunting small coding jobs. The live systems on the products page are company software. Work still in progress is labeled in development. Experiments stay experiments.

How we talk about the work

The insights are production notes: what broke for a user, and what the experience became after the fix. They are not a walkthrough of proprietary systems. They are also not a claim that every product is finished. TrustLoopAI, for example, is a real project in development — not a live commercial site.

If you want the commissioned version of this work, start a project. If you want to see the software, open the products.
